06 — FAQ

Good questions.

The short answers. For anything else, just ask — that's what the inquiry form is for.

How far in advance should I order?

Celebration cakes: at least two weeks is ideal. Wedding and large event cakes: two to three months. In a pinch? Ask anyway — if there's a gap in the calendar, we'll take it.

How many servings do I need?

Plan on one serving per guest, then round up — nobody has ever complained about leftover cake. Tell us your headcount in the inquiry form and we'll recommend a size.

Do you deliver?

Yes — we deliver across Virginia Beach and the Hampton Roads area, and we set up tiered and wedding cakes on site. Delivery fees depend on distance and are quoted up front.

Do you require a deposit?

A deposit reserves your date on the calendar, with the balance due at pickup or delivery. Your quote will spell out the exact amounts — no fine print.

Can you handle allergies or dietary needs?

Ask us. We offer options for common dietary needs and take allergies seriously in how we bake — tell us in the inquiry form and we'll talk through what's possible.

Do you offer tastings?

Tastings are available for wedding orders so you can choose flavors with confidence. Mention it in your inquiry and we'll set one up.
